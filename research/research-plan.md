# SPACE RELICS - Research Plan

## Research Objective

Document human-made objects left on the Moon and Mars.

## Research Questions

1. What objects were left behind?
2. Which NASA missions left them?
3. Why were they left?
4. Where are they located?
5. What scientific or historical value do they have?

## Research Sources

- NASA official websites
- NASA Planetary Data System
- NASA Image and Video Library

## Research Status

In progress.
